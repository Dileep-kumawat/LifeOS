## 2. Items Under "Needs Human Review"

1. **Calendar Week / Day View Horizontal Pinch / Pan**: The current week view renders all 7 days in a grid. On screens < 600px, 7 columns compress into ~45px per day, making time slots very narrow. It functions, but a 3-day or 1-day swipeable view would provide a superior native feel. Keeping 7-day grid with horizontal scroll container for now to avoid altering features/routes.
2. **TipTap Floating Bubble Menu**: The floating bubble menu on text selection can occasionally be obscured by virtual keyboards in Android WebViews. Making the fixed top/bottom toolbar sticky and horizontally scrollable solves 95% of formatting needs without relying on the floating bubble menu.
3. **Bottom Navigation Tab Count**: The bottom navigation has 5 primary items (Home, Schedule, Habits, Notes, More). On 320px viewports, 5 tabs leave exactly 64px per tab. Labels are 10px text and icons are 20px, which fits, but leaves no room for additional tabs without scrolling or dropping labels.
